## Color Psychology Applied

### Pine (#294A3A)
- Calm, growth, nature
- Primary actions, success, progress
- Used sparingly for emphasis

### Clay (#C96B4B)
- Warm, human, attention
- Errors, warnings, important alerts
- Not as primary brand color

### Sage (#A8BFA8)
- Friendly, AI, subtle
- AI features only
- Not main navigation
- Friendly green, not tech

### Ink (#202421)
- Professional, grounded, serious
- Text, headings, borders
- Not as background

### Paper (#F7F5F0)
- Warm, book-like, comfortable
- Backgrounds, cards
- Learning-focused warmth

---

## Typography as Brand Voice

### Serif Headings (Instrument Serif)
- Editorial, authoritative, human
- Page titles, course titles
- Feels like books, learning, tradition

### Sans UI (DM Sans)
- Modern, clean, approachable
- Body text, UI elements, labels
- Readable, neutral, functional

### Uppercase Labels
- Structured, organized, systematic
- Categories, metadata
- Premium feel

---

## Anti-Patterns Avoided

### ❌ AI Dashboard Feel
- No AI as hero
- No tech/purple colors
- No data visualization overload
- No command-based UI
- No robot-focused copy

### ❌ Over-Designed
- No card overuse
- No heavy shadows
- No gradients
- No heavy rounding
- No trendy effects

### ❌ Distracting
- No flashing elements
- No auto-playing videos
- No pop-ups
- No notifications overload
- No chaotic layouts

### ❌ Impersonal
- Instructor presence maintained
- AI-first workflows avoided
- Human touch preserved
- Conversational, not robotic

---

## Copywriting Guidelines Applied

### Voice: Intelligent, Supportive, Human

**Used**:
- "Your learning journey"
- "Continue where you left off"
- "Ask me anything about this lesson"
- "Recommended for you"
- "You're making great progress"

**Avoided**:
- "AI-powered learning"
- "Machine learning"
- "Neural networks"
- "Algorithmic recommendations"
- "Data-driven insights"
